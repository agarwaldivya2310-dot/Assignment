Asian Games 2026

The 2026 Asian Games is a multi-sport event held from September 19 to October 4, 2026 in Aichi Prefecture and Nagoya, Japan. India makes its 12th appearance at the competition. The Indian contingent for this edition consists of 503 athletes: 269 men and 234 women.
